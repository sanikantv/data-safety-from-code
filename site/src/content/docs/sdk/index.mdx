Most data an Android app collects comes from its SDKs. Each page below lists what one SDK collects or shares for Google Play's Data safety form, and the exact CSV rows it sets. 13 of the 21 entries come from the vendor's own Play data disclosure; the rest are marked as inferred.

Missing an SDK? Entries live in [`knowledge/sdks.yaml`](https://github.com/sanikantv/data-safety-from-code/blob/main/skills/data-safety-from-code/knowledge/sdks.yaml): open a pull request with a link to the vendor's disclosure page.

## Analytics

- [**Firebase Analytics**](/sdk/firebase-analytics/): Device or other IDs, Approximate location, App interactions
- [**Firebase Performance Monitoring**](/sdk/firebase-perf/): Diagnostics, Device or other IDs
- [**Facebook SDK**](/sdk/facebook-sdk/): App interactions, Device or other IDs, Crash logs (inferred)
- [**Mixpanel Android SDK**](/sdk/mixpanel-android/): App interactions, Device or other IDs, Approximate location (inferred)

## Crash reporting

- [**Firebase Crashlytics**](/sdk/firebase-crashlytics/): Crash logs, Diagnostics, Device or other IDs
- [**Sentry Android SDK**](/sdk/sentry-android/): Crash logs, Diagnostics, Device or other IDs (inferred)

## Sign-in

- [**Firebase Authentication**](/sdk/firebase-auth/): User IDs
- [**Sign in with Google / Credential Manager**](/sdk/google-sign-in/): depends on your use (inferred)
