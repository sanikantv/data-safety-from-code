## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- First version.
- `scan.py`: manifests (including merged release manifests), Groovy and Kotlin DSL Gradle files, version catalogs and bundles, `./gradlew dependencies` output, network security config, plain-HTTP URLs, and 20 kinds of source signals.
- `draft.py`, `fill_csv.py`, `validate.py`, `report.py`: a declaration becomes a CSV that keeps Google's template byte for byte; every conditional question is validated; the report links each answer to its evidence and lists changes against your current form.
- Knowledge base: 21 SDK entries (12 from official vendor disclosure pages) and 29 permissions.
- Tests: four fixture apps with golden CSVs, a byte-identical round trip of a real Play Console export, and validator failure cases.
- This site, with a page for every SDK entry generated from the knowledge base.
