**Short answer:** you still have to complete the form and give a privacy policy URL. Answer
**No** to "Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?" and the data
type and usage sections stay empty. That answer is only right if nothing, including any SDK,
sends user data off the device.

## What Google requires

Google's [Data safety help page](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469)
says apps that collect no user data must still complete the form. It also says a privacy policy
is required to complete the form, and that the form and policy can say no data is collected or
shared. Apps that are only on an internal testing track are exempt.

## Steps

1. **Confirm nothing leaves the device.** Check the checklist below.
2. In Play Console, open **App content** and select **Start** under Data safety.
3. Answer **No** to the collect-or-share question.
4. Add your **privacy policy URL** in Play Console. Say plainly that the app collects and shares
   no user data.
5. Check the store listing preview, then submit.

## What makes "no data" untrue

Collection means data is sent off the device, by your code or by any SDK in the app. Check for:

- **Ads SDKs.** AdMob's disclosure lists device or other IDs, approximate location, app
  interactions and diagnostics, collected and shared.
- **Analytics and crash reporting.** Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry and similar SDKs
  send identifiers, events or crash data.
- **Firebase services pulled in indirectly.** Messaging, Performance Monitoring and Remote Config
  depend on Firebase Installations, which sends an installation ID.
- **On-device ML that still reports.** Google's ML Kit disclosure says its on-device APIs send
  metadata such as device and app info and identifiers for diagnostics and usage analytics,
  though not the images or text you process.
- **In-app purchases** that your app or a service verifies or stores off the device: purchase
  history.
- **Any HTTP call** that carries user input, identifiers or location.
- **A WebView** that loads your own pages with analytics on them.
- **Sign-in.** If users can create an account, the app collects at least an account ID.

What does **not** count: data kept in a local database, files or preferences, and data processed
only on the device.

## CSV IDs

- `PSL_DATA_COLLECTION_COLLECTS_PERSONAL_DATA`: set to false.
- Every `PSL_DATA_TYPES_*` row and every `PSL_DATA_USAGE_RESPONSES:*` row stays blank.
- If your app has no accounts, `PSL_ACM_NONE` under `PSL_SUPPORTED_ACCOUNT_CREATION_METHODS` is
  the matching choice.

A CSV that says no data is collected but selects a data type contradicts itself.

## Common mistakes

- **Skipping the form** because the app is offline. It is still required.
- **No privacy policy** because "there's nothing to put in it". Google requires one.
- **Forgetting a library.** A single analytics or crash SDK means the app collects data.
- **Only checking direct dependencies.** Libraries can bring in other SDKs. Run
  `./gradlew :app:dependencies` to see the full tree.
- **Declaring data because of a permission.** A permission alone doesn't mean collection. If the
  permission isn't needed, remove it.
